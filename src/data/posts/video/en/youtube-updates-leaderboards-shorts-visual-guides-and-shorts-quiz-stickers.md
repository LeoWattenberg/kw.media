## Latest YouTube Updates: AI Tools, Live Stream Experiments, and Shorts Enhancements

YouTube continues to roll out a series of updates designed to streamline the creative process and increase viewer engagement across different formats. From AI-driven inspiration tools to refined visual guides for Shorts, these changes aim to help Creators optimize their content for both visibility and interactivity.

### AI Inspiration Tab Expanding in Europe

For Creators based in the European Union, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, there is an update regarding the launch window for the YouTube AI inspiration tab. This tool is designed to assist Creators in generating ideas and planning content using artificial intelligence.

The rollout process has already begun and is scheduled to be completed by June 2nd. If you are operating within these regions, it is recommended to keep a close eye on your dashboard and settings to see when the feature becomes available for your account.

### Enhancing Live Stream Engagement with Community Leaderboards

YouTube is currently experimenting with new ways to foster competition and community during [live broadcasts](/en/live/). The first wave of community leaderboards for live streams has begun rolling out. 

These leaderboards are part of an experimental phase, with the current test set to run for three weeks. Following this initial period, another experiment is expected to launch shortly after. Because these features are in the testing phase, availability may vary, as YouTube often uses a limited group of channels to gather data before a wider release.

In conjunction with these experiments, there will be a return to YouTube Creator Q&A live streams. These sessions provide a direct line of communication for Creators to get their specific questions answered regarding platform functionality and [growth strategies](/en/creator/).

### Improving Shorts Layouts with Visual Guides

One of the most practical updates for mobile creators is the introduction of visual guides within the [Shorts creation tool](/en/youtube-tips/) in the main YouTube app. This feature addresses a common pain point: placing text or stickers in areas where they are obscured by the user interface (UI).

When moving a sticker or text element around the screen, the app will now display striped areas. These zones indicate where interface elements—such as the like button, comment icon, or channel attribution—are located. By providing these visual boundaries, YouTube allows Creators to ensure that their on-screen text remains fully visible and readable for all viewers, regardless of the device they are using. This feature is live now; ensuring your app is updated to the latest version will grant you access to these guides.

### Turning Polls into Quizzes: New Shorts Sticker Functionality

Shorts interactivity has received another boost with an update to [poll stickers](/youtube-tips-en/new-poll-stickers-for-shorts/). While poll stickers have previously allowed Creators to gather opinions, they can now be transformed into quiz stickers.

By marking a specific answer as "correct," the poll effectively becomes a quiz. However, there is a notable technical limitation: because this feature is built upon the existing poll sticker framework, it only supports two possible answers. 

From a design perspective, this creates a 50/50 chance for the viewer to guess the correct answer. While this limits the complexity of the quiz, it still offers a quick way to engage viewers and test their knowledge in a fast-paced format. Creators can use these to drive engagement by challenging their audience or sparking discussions in the comments about the topic at hand.

### Summary of Key Changes

Whether you are focusing on long-form live content or short-form vertical video, these updates provide new tools for optimization:

*   **Regional AI Access:** EU, UK, and Switzerland creators should see the AI inspiration tab by June 2nd.
*   **Live Interaction:** Keep an eye out for community leaderboards during your streams to increase viewer competition.
*   **Shorts Precision:** Use the new striped visual guides to avoid UI overlap when placing text.
*   **Interactive Shorts:** Experiment with the updated poll stickers to create simple two-option quizzes.
