## Why Your YouTube Handle Matters

[YouTube has transitioned from using traditional display names to @ handles](/youtube-tips-en/what-are-youtube-handles-aliases/), particularly within live chat environments. If you have not yet customized your handle, you likely have a default identifier—such as `user123`—which can make your account appear like a bot to other users and Creators. 

To ensure you look authentic and professional when interacting with the community, it is essential to replace these generic defaults with a personalized handle that represents your identity.

## How to Change Your Handle on Mobile

Updating your identity does not require a desktop computer; you can manage this process directly within the YouTube mobile app. Follow these steps to update your handle:

1. **Open the YouTube App:** Launch the application on your mobile device.
2. **Access Your Profile:** Tap your profile picture, located in the bottom right corner of the screen.
3. **Navigate to Your Channel:** Select **View Channel** to enter your personal channel page.
4. **Enter Edit Mode:** Tap the pencil icon (the edit button) to open your customization settings.
5. **Modify the Handle:** Tap on the **Handle** section to begin editing.

### Choosing a Unique Identifier

When selecting a new handle, keep in mind that it must be unique across the platform. You are permitted to use between three and 30 characters. While underscores and dots are supported for formatting, it is recommended to keep the handle clean to ensure it remains readable for others in chat.

## Important Limitations and Warnings

Before you finalize your changes, be aware of YouTube's restrictions regarding handle updates. You can only change your handle twice every 14 days. 

This limitation makes accuracy critical; if you save your handle with a spelling mistake or a typo, you may be stuck with that error for up to two weeks before you are eligible to change it again. Always double-check your spelling and character choices before hitting the save button.

### Finalizing Your Update

Once you have verified your new handle, hit save. Your updated @ handle will now be active, allowing you to jump back into live chats and engage with the community without looking like an automated account. For those who prefer managing their account from a computer, there is also a guide on [how to change your YouTube handle via desktop](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-change-youtube-handle-desktop/).
