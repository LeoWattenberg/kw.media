## Understanding YouTube Hype: A New Path to Discoverability

For emerging creators, the hardest part of growth is often breaking through the noise to reach new audiences. To address this, YouTube introduced [YouTube Hype](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-expands-free-hype-to-the-eu-10-year-member-badges/), a feature designed specifically to give channels in their growth phase an extra push in visibility. 

Rather than relying solely on the standard recommendation algorithm, Hype functions as a community-driven voting system. It allows viewers to actively support a creator's recent work, potentially pushing that content onto country-specific leaderboards where it can be discovered by users who may not have encountered the channel otherwise.

### Who is Eligible for YouTube Hype?

[YouTube Hype](/youtube-tips-en/tax-form-updates-for-youtube-partners-and-youtube-hype-under-fire/) is not available to every creator or viewer; it is targeted specifically at "emerging" talent. To qualify as a creator, you must meet the following criteria:

*   **Subscriber Count:** Your channel should generally have between 500 and 500,000 subscribers.
*   **Program Status:** You must be part of the [YouTube Partner Programme](/en/creator/).
*   **Content Type:** The feature applies to long-form videos.

There is also a strict time constraint: the Hype button only appears on videos uploaded within the last seven days. This ensures that the leaderboards remain current and that the "push" is applied to fresh content rather than legacy videos.

From the viewer's perspective, there is one important technical caveat: Hype can only be performed using a personal account. Brand accounts are currently unable to use this feature.

### How the Hype System Works

At its core, the free Hype action is a gesture of support that costs the viewer nothing. While creators generally cannot see exactly which individual users hyped their videos, the collective effort moves the video up the rankings.

The most significant aspect of this system is how YouTube calculates "points." Based on available data, YouTube does not treat every Hype equally; instead, it weights them based on the size of the channel. 

#### The Weighting System and Bonus Tiers
Data suggests that a Hype from a viewer on a smaller channel is worth more points than a Hype for a larger channel. This weighting prevents channels at the higher end of the eligibility bracket (closer to 500,000 subscribers) from completely dominating the leaderboards simply due to their larger existing audience.

Furthermore, there appear to be "hidden bonus tiers" tied to specific subscriber thresholds—likely around 5,000 and 25,000 subscribers. While the overall subscriber count sets the general curve of how points are awarded, hitting these specific tiers may heavily influence the final point value of each Hype, providing a significant boost to creators at those specific stages of growth.

### Experimental Features: Paid Hype

While the standard Hype feature is free, YouTube has been experimenting with "paid hype" in specific markets, such as Brazil and Turkey. In these regions, viewers have the option to purchase additional hype levels.

The data indicates that paid Hype acts as a multiplier for the base Hype value. This creates an interesting synergy for smaller creators: because they already receive more base points per Hype due to the weighting system, a paid Hype is mathematically more valuable for a small channel than it is for a larger one. If this feature expands, it could eventually mirror the functionality of Super Thanks, turning community support into a direct tool for algorithmic promotion.

### Strategic Implementation for Creators

It is important to view YouTube Hype as a signal rather than a "magic growth button." Simply uploading a video and hoping viewers find the Hype button is unlikely to yield significant results. Instead, creators should integrate Hype into their broader community strategy:

1.  **Focus on Engagement:** Since Hype relies on motivated viewers, the priority should be building a highly engaged core audience that feels invested in your growth.
2.  **Optimize the Eligibility Window:** Because the feature only lasts for seven days post-upload, any calls to action (CTAs) regarding Hype must happen immediately after the video goes live.
3.  **Educate Your Audience:** Many viewers may not be aware of the Hype button or how it differs from a Like. When it makes sense—such as during a particularly high-effort project—letting your community know that they can help you reach a leaderboard can motivate them to act.

By treating Hype as an additional tool in the toolkit rather than a primary growth strategy, emerging creators can leverage their most loyal fans to increase their discoverability across their region.
