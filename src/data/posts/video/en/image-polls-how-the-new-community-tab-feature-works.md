## Enhancing Audience Engagement with Image Polls

For Creators who have already enabled the [YouTube Community tab](/youtube-tips-en/expand-your-reach-stay-in-touch-with-your-community/), there is a powerful way to increase interaction through [image polls](/youtube-tips-en/new-poll-stickers-for-shorts/). While traditional text polls are effective for quick questions, image polls allow you to replace standard text lines with visual options, making your posts more eye-catching and engaging within the Community feed.

## How Image Polls Work on Android

At the time of this feature's introduction, Creators can utilize their [Android smartphones](/en/tools/) to build these interactive posts. Instead of typing out multiple-choice answers, you can upload images to represent each polling option. This visual shift transforms a simple query into a more dynamic experience for the viewer, as images often convey information faster and more effectively than text alone.

### Cross-Device Compatibility
While the creation process is focused on the Android mobile experience, the reach of these polls is universal. Once an image poll is published to your [Community tab](/en/youtube-tips/), your audience can interact with it normally across all devices. Whether your viewers are browsing on a desktop computer, an iOS device, or an Android phone, they will be able to see the images and cast their votes without issue.

## Driving Traffic via Video Linking

One of the most useful aspects of this feature is the ability to integrate your existing content directly into the poll. In addition to the visual voting options, you have the option to link one of your own videos to the post.

This functionality allows you to create a strategic bridge between community engagement and video consumption. For example, you can use an image poll to ask viewers which topic they prefer or which thumbnail they like best, and then provide a link to a related video that provides deeper context or serves as the subject of the poll. This not only encourages interaction but also helps drive traffic back to your video library.

## Practical Implementation for Creators

To make the most of this tool, ensure that your [Community tab](/youtube-tips-en/everything-new-in-youtube-studio-mobile-content-and-community-tab-updates/) is active and accessible on your account. When creating a new post via the Android app:

1. Select the poll option from the community post menu.
2. Choose the image-based format to replace text lines with visuals.
3. Upload clear, representative images for each choice to avoid confusion.
4. Optionally, attach a link to a relevant video to guide your audience toward more of your content.

By shifting from text-only polls to visual ones, you can create a more stimulating environment for your community and leverage the visual nature of the platform to increase overall participation.
