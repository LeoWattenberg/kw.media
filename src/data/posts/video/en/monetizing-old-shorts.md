## Activating Revenue for Your Existing Shorts

If you have already [created Shorts](/youtube-tips-en/creating-shorts-on-youtube-youtube-tutorial-shorts-2/), it is important to ensure that monetization is actively enabled for those videos. Many creators focus on the creative process of producing short-form content but may overlook the administrative settings required to actually earn from their work.

Without explicitly activating this feature, you will not be able to receive a revenue share from your views. This means that even if your Shorts are performing well and gaining significant traction, the potential earnings remain locked until the monetization setting is toggled on.

### The Importance of Revenue Share Activation

Monetization for Shorts operates differently than traditional long-form video ads. Because the system relies on a revenue share model, ensuring your content is opted into this system is the only way to start generating income from your existing library. If you uploaded your content before these features were fully implemented or if you have various settings across your channel, it is highly recommended to perform a manual check of your video list.

### How to Monetize Your Shorts in Bulk

Updating every single video individually can be an exhausting and time-consuming process, especially for creators with a large portfolio of content. Fortunately, [YouTube Studio](/en/youtube-tips/) provides a bulk editing tool that allows you to manage multiple videos simultaneously. 

By using this feature, you can apply monetization settings to your entire library of Shorts in just a few clicks, ensuring no video is left unmonetized.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Bulk Monetization

To activate revenue for your old Shorts, follow these practical steps within the platform:

1. **Access YouTube Studio:** Log into your account and navigate to the YouTube Studio dashboard.
2. **Filter Your Content:** Go to the "Content" section and filter your videos to show only your Shorts.
3. **Select Your Videos:** Use the selection box to select all the Shorts you wish to monetize. You can choose specific videos or select every Short in your library at once.
4. **Apply Monetization:** Once selected, use the bulk edit menu to find the monetization option and set it to "On."

Once these steps are completed, your existing content will be eligible for revenue share. For those wondering about the broader rollout of these features, you can find more details on [when short monetization is coming](/youtube-tips-en/when-is-the-short-monetization-coming/). By taking a few minutes to audit your settings in YouTube Studio, you can ensure that all your hard work is properly rewarded.
