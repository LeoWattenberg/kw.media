## Transcript

With the new [YouTube channel guidelines feature](/youtube-tips-en/new-feature-alert-channel-guidelines/), you can easily explain your community rules to new viewers and chatters. To add channel guidelines, open the Community tab in your [YouTube Studio settings](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-receive-youtube-redirects-youtube-raids-on-your-channel/), then switch to Channel guidelines. You can provide an introductory message for your viewers and fill out three text boxes for custom rules.
