## Setting Up Your YouTube Channel Guidelines

Maintaining a healthy and welcoming community is essential for any Creator. To help manage viewer behavior and set clear expectations, the [YouTube channel guidelines feature](/youtube-tips-en/new-feature-alert-channel-guidelines/) allows you to communicate your specific community rules directly to your audience. This tool is particularly useful for onboarding new viewers and chatters, ensuring they understand the standards of your channel before they begin interacting.

### How to Configure Your Guidelines

Setting up these guidelines is handled within the backend of your channel management tools. To get started, follow these steps:

1. Log into your account and open the [YouTube Studio settings](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-receive-youtube-redirects-youtube-raids-on-your-channel/).
2. Navigate to the **Community** tab.
3. Switch to the **Channel guidelines** section.

Once you are in this menu, you have access to the specific fields needed to define your community's boundaries and culture.

### Customizing Your Community Rules

The channel guidelines feature is divided into two main components: an introductory message and a set of custom rules.

#### The Introductory Message
The first step is providing an introductory message. This serves as the opening greeting for viewers and chatters. It is your opportunity to set the overall tone of your community—whether that is professional, casual, or strictly moderated—before the viewer reads the specific rules.

#### Custom Community Rules
Beyond the introduction, YouTube provides three dedicated text boxes for custom rules. Because space is limited to these three slots, it is important to be concise and clear. These fields allow you to explicitly state what behavior is expected in your community and what is not permitted. 

By filling out these guidelines, you create a transparent framework for interaction. This reduces ambiguity for new arrivals and provides a reference point for moderation, helping you maintain the specific atmosphere you want for your YouTube channel.
