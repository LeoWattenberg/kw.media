## Understanding YouTube Live View Hours and YPP Requirements

For many Creators, reaching the necessary watch-time threshold is one of the most challenging hurdles when applying for the [YouTube Partner Program](/youtube-tips-en/everything-important-about-the-new-youtube-fan-funding-partner-level/). With various content formats available—from short-form clips to long-form uploads and real-time broadcasts—a common question arises: Do hours accumulated during a YouTube Live stream count toward the [public watch hour requirement](/en/youtube-tips/)?

The short answer is yes. Public live streams can contribute to your total public watch hours, provided they adhere to specific platform criteria. However, not every minute streamed is automatically added to your monetization progress. Understanding the distinction between "valid public watch hours" and other types of views is essential for any Creator planning their growth strategy.

## The Conditions for Valid Live Stream Hours

While [live streaming](/en/live/) can be a powerful tool to accelerate your progress toward the [YouTube Partner Program](/en/creator/), the hours only count if the content remains accessible to the general public. 

### The Requirement for Public Visibility
The most critical factor is the visibility setting of the stream. To count toward YPP requirements, the live stream must be set to "Public." If a stream is broadcast as "Unlisted" or "Private," the watch time generated during that session will not contribute to your public watch hour total. 

### The Importance of Archiving
The journey of a live stream doesn't end when you hit the "End Stream" button. For those hours to remain credited to your account, the stream must be saved as a public video archive. If a Creator deletes the recording after the broadcast or changes its visibility from Public to Private or Unlisted, the watch time associated with that video is removed from the YPP counter. 

In essence, for live streaming to serve as a viable path toward monetization, the content must remain discoverable and viewable by anyone on the platform both during and after the live event.

## What Does Not Count Toward Watch Hours?

To avoid confusion and potential setbacks in your application process, it is important to identify which types of views are excluded from the public watch hour metric.

### Private and Unlisted Content
As mentioned, any content that is not "Public" is excluded. This includes:
*   **Private Videos:** Content restricted only to the Creator.
*   **Unlisted Videos:** Content that can only be viewed by those with a direct link.
*   **Deleted Content:** Once a video is removed from the platform, its accumulated watch time is no longer counted toward YPP requirements.

### The Distinction of YouTube Shorts
It is also vital to distinguish between long-form live content and [Shorts views](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-get-into-the-partner-program-with-youtube-shorts/). While both are forms of video content, YouTube tracks them using different metrics. Views from the Shorts feed do not count toward the traditional "public watch hours" requirement; instead, they fall under a separate set of requirements specifically designed for the Shorts format.

## Practical Strategies for Creators

If you intend to use YouTube Live as a primary method for qualifying for the Partner Program, your approach should be strategic rather than purely quantitative. Simply streaming for long periods is not enough; the content must be engaging enough to retain viewers.

### Focus on Viewer Value
To maximize your watch hours, focus on providing real value to your audience. Whether through educational tutorials, interactive Q&A sessions, or community-driven events, high-value content encourages longer average view durations. The more time a viewer spends engaged in your stream, the faster you will reach your goals.

### Maintain Your Archives
Once a stream is finished, treat the archive as a permanent piece of your channel's library. Instead of hiding the video, consider optimizing the title and description to make it searchable for new viewers. This allows the video to continue generating "passive" watch hours long after the live broadcast has ended.

### Consistency and Planning
Integrating live streams into your regular upload schedule can create a predictable rhythm for your community. By announcing stream dates via Community Posts or other channel updates, you can ensure a higher baseline of concurrent viewers, which directly impacts the speed at which you accumulate valid public watch hours.
