## Expanding Your Identity: YouTube Updates @handles and Language Support

YouTube is making significant updates to how Creators identify themselves on the platform. The most notable change comes to @handles, which are designed to provide a unique, easy-to-share identifier for every channel. To better support a global community of creators, YouTube has expanded language support and modified the character rules for these handles.

### Multilingual Support for @handles

In an effort to increase inclusivity and accessibility, @handles now support letters and numbers across 75 different languages. This expansion allows Creators from diverse linguistic backgrounds to create handles that feel more natural and authentic to their identity and target audience, rather than being forced to rely solely on Latin characters.

However, this expansion comes with some specific adjustments regarding character length. While the standard limit for many handles is 30 characters, YouTube has implemented shorter limits for certain scripts due to the nature of those alphabets. Specifically, if you are using the following alphabets, your handle length is now restricted to either 10 or 20 characters:

*   Arabic
*   Ethiopian
*   Hiragana
*   Katakana

Creators using these languages should keep these limits in mind when planning their branding or [updating their handles](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-change-youtube-handle-desktop/) to ensure their desired name fits within the new constraints. For more detailed information on how to manage your handle, you can refer to the official [YouTube Help Center](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11585688).

### New Special Characters for Customization

Beyond language support, YouTube is introducing more flexibility in how handles are constructed by allowing new special characters. Previously, options were more limited, but Creators can now incorporate the following into their @handles:

*   Underscores (`_`)
*   Hyphens (`-`)
*   Periods (`.`)
*   Latin interpuncts

The addition of these characters allows for better readability and a wider variety of naming conventions. The Latin interpunct, in particular, is a less common character but is now officially permitted and already appearing in use across the platform. These additions give you more tools to differentiate your brand if your preferred alphanumeric handle is already taken.

## Premium Feature Rollouts: Jump Ahead for Android

For those who invest in YouTube Premium, there is a significant update regarding the "Jump Ahead" feature. This tool is designed to help viewers skip directly to the most relevant or interesting parts of a video, improving the overall viewing experience.

Previously limited to specific regions, Jump Ahead is now 100% available for all Android devices worldwide. If you are a Premium subscriber using an Android device, this feature should now be active regardless of your geographic location, moving beyond its initial US-centric rollout.

## Optimizing Performance with "Test & Compare"

One of the most powerful tools for [channel growth](/en/creator/) is the ability to optimize click-through rates (CTR) through thumbnail experimentation. YouTube's A/B testing tool, known as "Test & Compare," has now been unlocked for 100% of Creators who have access to advanced features.

### What is Test & Compare?

Test & Compare allows you to upload multiple versions of a thumbnail for a single video. YouTube then serves these different versions to different segments of your audience, collecting data on which one performs better. This removes the guesswork from thumbnail design, allowing you to make data-driven decisions based on actual viewer behavior.

### How to Access Thumbnail Testing

If you find that you cannot use the "Test & Compare" feature in your YouTube Studio, it is likely because you have not yet enabled "advanced features" for your account. These features typically require a certain level of channel history or identity verification. 

To start using A/B testing, we recommend checking your account settings to ensure that advanced features are fully unlocked. Once enabled, you can begin testing different imagery and text overlays to see which version drives the most engagement for your content.
