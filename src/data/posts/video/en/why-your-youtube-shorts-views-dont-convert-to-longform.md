## The Gap Between Shorts and Long-Form Views

It is a common and frustrating experience for many creators: your YouTube Shorts are exploding with thousands of views, yet your main long-form videos are barely registering any traffic—sometimes receiving as few as two views. When this happens, the immediate reaction is often to wonder if you should quit or if your content simply isn't good enough.

However, the problem usually isn't that your videos "suck." Instead, the issue lies in a fundamental misunderstanding of how different audiences behave within the same ecosystem.

## Two Platforms, One App

To solve this discrepancy, you must first recognize that Shorts and long-form videos are essentially two different platforms living inside the exact same app. While they share the same branding, they attract viewers with entirely different intents.

Shorts viewers are "swipe hungry." They are looking for quick, high-energy bursts of information or entertainment. Expecting this audience to suddenly pivot from a 60-second clip to a deep, 15-minute long-form video is an unrealistic expectation. The psychological state of someone scrolling through a feed is vastly different from someone who has intentionally decided to sit down and watch a comprehensive video.

## Identifying the Real Problem

When your main videos fail to gain traction despite your Shorts success, it is tempting to blame your [promotion strategy](/en/creator/). However, if your views are nearly non-existent, the promo isn't the primary issue. 

The problem typically resides in one of three areas:
*   **The Title:** It may not be compelling enough to trigger a click.
*   **The Thumbnail:** It might fail to visually communicate the value of the video.
*   **The Video Idea:** The core concept itself may not be something that an audience is currently interested in or searching for.

## Shifting Your Strategy to Avoid Burnout

The key to growth is to stop trying to force a conversion from viewers with short attention spans. Instead, you should focus on packaging your main content for people who are actually searching for those specific topics. 

By [optimizing your long-form content for search intent](/en/youtube-tips/) rather than relying on the spillover from Shorts, you align your videos with viewers who already have the desire to consume longer content. Failing to make this distinction often leads to a cycle of frustration and burnout, as creators spend hours on production only to wonder why they aren't [getting the views they expected](/youtube-tips-en/why-50-hours-of-editing-gets-0-views/).
