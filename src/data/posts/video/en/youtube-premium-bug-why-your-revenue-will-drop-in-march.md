## YouTube Premium Revenue Correction and Twitch Multistreaming Updates

If you notice a sudden dip in your earnings when [checking your analytics](/en/creator/) next month, there is a specific reason for it. This week's creator news covers a significant revenue correction on YouTube, a major policy shift for multistreamers on Twitch, and several smaller UI updates to the YouTube ecosystem.

## The YouTube Premium Revenue Bug: What Creators Need to Know

YouTube is currently notifying a subset of creators via email about a "premium revenue overpayment correction." This situation stems from a technical bug in YouTube's system that accidentally attributed an inflated amount of YouTube Premium watch time to certain videos. Because Premium revenue is tied directly to how much time Premium members spend watching content, this error resulted in some creators receiving higher payments than they were actually entitled to.

### The Timeline for Correction
For those affected, the correction process follows a specific timeline:
*   **March 1st:** YouTube will begin fixing the bug and gradually phasing out the overpayments.
*   **March through April:** The transition period where revenue numbers will slowly adjust.
*   **May 1st:** By this date, analytics will fully reflect the corrected, lower figures.

The most important takeaway for creators is that **YouTube is not clawing back the money.** Any overpayments received prior to the fix are yours to keep; you will not see a negative balance or a request for repayment. However, you should expect your monthly revenue to decrease as the system returns to accuracy.

### Assessing the Financial Impact
While any drop in income can be alarming, the actual impact on most channels is likely to be minimal. To put this into perspective, kw.media analyzed a full year of 2025 data across five different channels within its portfolio—spanning diverse niches including automotive, finance, and gaming.

The data revealed that [YouTube Premium revenue](/youtube-tips-en/new-audio-settings-vpn-revenue-warning-youtube-creator-news/) accounts for an average of only 4.13% of total channel revenue. Because the bug only affected a portion of that small percentage, the resulting dip in your RPM (Revenue Per Mille) should be slight. If you see a minor decrease in your earnings starting in March, it is likely due to this correction rather than a drop in performance or ad demand.

## Twitch Greenlights Combined Chat for Multistreamers

For creators who stream across multiple platforms simultaneously, the technical and legal landscape has just become much friendlier. Historically, using a combined chat—where messages from YouTube and Twitch are merged into a single feed—was a "gray area." Due to strict exclusivity rules and platform competition, some creators feared that displaying a unified chat could lead to penalties or violations of their terms of service.

### A Shift in Policy
Twitch CEO Dan Clancy has recently clarified the platform's stance in his patch notes: there will be no enforcement or penalties for creators who use combined chats on stream. This is a significant quality-of-life improvement, as it removes the anxiety surrounding exclusivity and allows creators to engage with their entire community regardless of which platform the viewer is using.

### Essential Tools for Unified Chat
To implement this workflow, creators can utilize specialized tools that bridge the gap between platforms. Two highly recommended options are [Social Stream Ninja](https://socialstream.ninja/) and [Streamer.bot](https://streamer.bot/). 

By linking your YouTube and Twitch accounts through these services, you gain several operational advantages:
*   **Unified Viewing:** Read all incoming messages in one window rather than switching between tabs.
*   **Simultaneous Interaction:** Reply to your community across both platforms at once.
*   **OBS Integration:** You can set up the combined chat as an OBS browser source overlay, allowing your viewers on both platforms to see a merged conversation on screen.
*   **Centralized Moderation:** Manage and moderate your chat from one central hub, reducing the overhead of managing multiple moderation teams or windows.

For any creator currently multistreaming, implementing this infrastructure is highly recommended to improve viewer engagement and streamline the broadcasting process.

## YouTube Live and UI Updates

Beyond revenue and streaming policies, YouTube has introduced a few changes to its interface and live features that may affect your daily workflow.

### Vertical Live Chat Translations
As vertical video continues to grow in importance for mobile audiences, YouTube is enhancing the experience for vertical live streams. YouTube now offers vertical live chat translations. If you are streaming in a vertical format, messages from viewers will be automatically translated into your set language based on your account settings. 

This feature is particularly useful for creators building a global audience, as it allows you to communicate with international viewers in real-time without needing a dedicated translator. To ensure this works correctly, double-check that your preferred language settings are accurately configured in your account.

### Subscription Tab: Grid View Permanence
On the web version of YouTube, a minor but noticeable UI change has occurred regarding the subscription tab. YouTube has deprecated the "list view" option. Previously, creators and viewers could toggle between a grid layout and a list layout to browse subscriptions; however, the platform has now made **grid view permanent**. 

If your dashboard suddenly looks more cluttered or different than usual, this is the reason. While it is a small change, it removes a layer of customization for those who preferred the streamlined nature of the list view.

### Summary Checklist for Creators
*   **Check your email:** Look for notifications regarding YouTube Premium overpayments so you aren't surprised by March analytics.
*   **Review Revenue Splits:** Check your YouTube Studio analytics to see what percentage of your income comes from Premium members.
*   **Update Stream Setup:** If you multistream, explore Social Stream Ninja or Streamer.bot to take advantage of the new Twitch chat policy.
*   **Verify Language Settings:** Ensure your language preferences are correct to benefit from vertical live chat translations.
