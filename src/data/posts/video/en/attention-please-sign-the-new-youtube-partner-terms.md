## Mandatory Contract Update for YouTube Partners

YouTube has issued a critical update regarding the legal agreements that govern the [YouTube Partner Program](/youtube-tips-en/everything-important-about-the-new-youtube-fan-funding-partner-level/). For many creators, this means a new requirement to review and sign an amended base contract to ensure uninterrupted access to the platform's partner features.

### Who is affected by this update?

This specific contractual amendment does not apply to every user on the platform. It is targeted specifically at Creators who meet the following two criteria:

1.  **Existing Partners:** You must already be a member of the [YouTube Partner Program](/en/creator/).
2.  **Non-Music Creators:** This update is intended for general content creators. Those categorized as music Creators are excluded from this specific requirement, as their agreements typically follow different structures.

If you fall into these categories, it is essential to check your account notifications and dashboard for the amended agreement.

### The Requirement: Signing the Amended Base Contract

The "base contract" serves as the primary legal foundation between a Creator and YouTube, outlining the rights, obligations, and revenue-sharing terms associated with monetization. An "amended" contract indicates that YouTube has modified certain terms of this original agreement.

To remain in good standing and maintain your status within the program, you are required to formally accept these new terms. This is not an optional update; it is a mandatory legal step to ensure your account remains compliant with current platform policies.

### Deadline and Consequences

Based on the publication context of this notice, the deadline for signing the updated terms was **July 10, 2023**. 

The implications of missing this deadline are significant. The transcript indicates that signing is necessary to "continue on YouTube" in the capacity of a partner. While you may still have a channel, failing to sign the amended contract could lead to:

*   **Loss of Partner Features:** You may lose access to the specific tools and privileges granted to members of the [YouTube Partner Program](/youtube-tips-en/new-objection-right-in-the-youtube-partner-program-and-channel-page-overhauls/).
*   **Monetization Risks:** Since the base contract governs how creators earn money, an unsigned agreement could jeopardize your ability to monetize your content.
*   **Account Status Issues:** Failure to comply with legal requirements can lead to administrative restrictions on your account.

### Practical Steps for Creators

If you are a non-music Creator in the Partner Program, you should:

*   **Check YouTube Studio:** Look for notifications or banners alerting you to pending legal documents.
*   **Review the Changes:** Carefully read the amended sections of the base contract to understand how the terms have changed.
*   **Sign Promptly:** Ensure the electronic signature process is completed before the specified cutoff date to avoid any disruption in your channel's operations.
