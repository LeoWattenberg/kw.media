## Understanding YouTube Gift Membership Refunds

[Gifting memberships](/youtube-tips-en/elevate-your-channel-with-memberships/) is a powerful way for viewers to support their favorite Creators and foster community growth during live streams. However, because these gifts depend on the presence of eligible recipients, there are specific rules governing when a transaction is finalized and when a refund is triggered.

## When Does a Refund Occur?

A refund is not automatic for every gifting attempt; it depends entirely on whether there are viewers available to receive the memberships. 

If YouTube cannot find any other active viewers to [gift memberships](/youtube-tips-en/how-it-works-youtube-membership-gifting/) to during a stream, the transaction cannot be completed as intended. In this specific scenario, the user who attempted to purchase the gifts will receive a [full refund](/youtube-tips-en/where-can-i-find-my-purchases-and-memberships-on-youtube/). This process typically takes one to two days to reflect in the user's account.

## Revenue Generation for Creators

For the Creator, the financial outcome is determined by whether at least one membership was successfully distributed. 

### Successful Gifting and Payouts
If even a single membership is successfully gifted to a viewer, the transaction is considered successful. In this case, the Creator immediately receives the revenue generated from the gift purchase. It is important to note that the Creator does not receive the gross amount; the payout consists of the total revenue minus the standard YouTube cut and applicable VAT (Value Added Tax).

### The Threshold for Revenue
The key takeaway for Creators is that they do not need every single gifted membership to be claimed to earn revenue from the transaction. As long as one gift finds a recipient, the revenue is processed for the Creator rather than being refunded to the gifter.

## How Memberships Are Distributed

When a user gifts memberships, YouTube does not limit the recipients only to those currently typing in the chat. To ensure the gifts are utilized, they are awarded to two primary groups:

*   **Active Chat Users:** Those currently engaging with the live stream in real-time.
*   **Offline Subscribers:** Loyal followers who are subscribed to the channel but may not be present during the specific broadcast.

This distribution method ensures that the generosity of a gifting user reaches as many members of the community as possible, maximizing the impact of the support provided to the Creator.
