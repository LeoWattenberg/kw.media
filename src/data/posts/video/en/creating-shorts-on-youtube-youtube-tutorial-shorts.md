## Getting Started with YouTube Shorts

YouTube Shorts provide an efficient way for creators to reach audiences through short-form vertical video. Because the process is integrated directly into the mobile experience, producing a Short is designed to be quick and intuitive. 

### Initiating the Creation Process

To begin creating your content, open the YouTube app on your mobile device. The entry point for all uploads and creations is the plus (+) symbol located in the app's navigation. Tapping this icon opens a menu of options; from here, select "[Create Short](/youtube-tips-en/different-ways-to-create-shorts-shorts/)". 

Choosing this specific option ensures that your video is formatted for the Shorts player, distinguishing it from a [standard long-form video upload](/en/creator/).

### Capturing Your Content

Once you have entered the Shorts creation interface, you have two primary methods for gathering your footage:

*   **Direct Recording:** You can record a new clip in real-time using your device's camera. This is ideal for spontaneous content or quick updates.
*   **Using Existing Clips:** If you have already filmed and edited a video on your mobile device, you can simply upload an existing clip from your gallery.

### Enhancing Your Video with Creative Tools

A raw video clip is often the starting point. To increase engagement and provide more context to your viewers, YouTube provides built-in tools to polish the final product:

*   **Adding Music:** Incorporating audio tracks allows you to set the mood of the video or lean into trending sounds that can help with discoverability.
*   **Adding Text:** Text overlays are essential for highlighting key points or ensuring the video remains understandable for viewers who may be watching with their sound turned off.

### Finalizing and Publishing

The final step in the production pipeline is the metadata. Before your Short goes live, you will be prompted to enter a title. A clear, concise title helps viewers understand the subject of your video and assists the platform in categorizing your content. Once the title is set, the process is complete, and your Short can be published.
