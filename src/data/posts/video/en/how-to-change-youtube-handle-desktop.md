## Why Your YouTube Live Chat Name Looks Like a Bot

If you have noticed that your name in YouTube live chat looks like a random string of characters—resembling a barcode or a bot—it is likely due to a recent update in YouTube's chat logic. 

Previously, [display names](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-change-youtube-handle-mobile/) were used to identify users in the chat. However, those have been replaced. Now, your identity in live interactions is tied exclusively to your [ad handle](/youtube-tips-en/what-are-youtube-handles-aliases/). If you haven't customized your handle, you might be appearing as a generic "user 123," which can make your account look automated rather than human.

To fix this and ensure you are recognizable to other Creators and viewers, you will need to [update your handle](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-update-handles-now-support-more-languages-and-special-characters/) via the desktop interface.

## How to Change Your YouTube Handle on Desktop

Updating your handle is a straightforward process managed through the backend of your channel. Follow these steps to change your identifier:

1. **Access YouTube Studio:** Navigate to studio.youtube.com and sign in to your account.
2. **Open Customization:** On the left-hand sidebar menu, locate and click on the **Customization** tab.
3. **Navigate to Basic Info:** Once inside the customization menu, click on the **Basic info** tab at the top of the page.
4. **Edit Your Handle:** You will see a field dedicated to your handle. Delete the existing text and enter a new, readable name that represents you or your brand.
5. **Save Changes:** After entering your new handle, click **Publish** to make the change live across the platform.

## Handle Requirements and Constraints

When choosing a new handle, YouTube enforces specific formatting rules. To ensure your new handle is accepted, keep the following guidelines in mind:

### Character Limits and Allowed Symbols
Your handle must be between **3 and 30 characters** long. You are permitted to use:
* Letters
* Numbers
* Underscores
* Dots

However, YouTube prohibits the use of URLs or phone numbers within your handle to prevent spam and maintain platform security.

### The Change Limit Warning
It is critical to double-check your spelling before hitting publish. YouTube limits how often you can modify this identifier: **you can only change your handle twice every 14 days.**

If you make a typo or change your mind immediately after publishing, you may be stuck with that error for up to two weeks before the system allows another update. Take a moment to verify the spelling so you can return to looking like a human in the chat without any further delays.
