## Simplifying Music Licensing with the YouTube Audio Library

Finding high-quality music for video production often comes with a significant hurdle: licensing. For many Creators, navigating copyright laws and securing the right to use specific tracks can be complex and time-consuming. To solve this, YouTube provides a built-in solution via the [Audio Library in YouTube Studio](/youtube-tips-en/new-developments-in-creator-music/).

### What is the YouTube Audio Library?

The YouTube Audio Library is a curated collection of royalty-free music and sound effects designed specifically for content creators. Instead of searching for external tracks and worrying about potential copyright strikes, Creators can access over 1,000 different music tracks directly within their dashboard. This resource allows you to find professional audio that fits the mood of your project without the need to negotiate individual licenses or pay expensive fees.

### Monetization and Copyright Safety

One of the most critical advantages of using this library is its compatibility with monetization. When you use tracks sourced from the YouTube Audio Library, you can [monetize your videos](/en/creator/) without the risk of copyright claims that might otherwise divert your earnings to a music rights holder. By utilizing these pre-cleared tracks, you ensure that your revenue remains yours while maintaining a high standard of audio production.

### Using Music Outside of YouTube

While the library is integrated into the YouTube ecosystem, its utility extends beyond the platform. You are permitted to download these tracks and use them in videos published on other platforms. However, there is an important legal caveat to keep in mind: the music must only be used as an accompaniment to your own original content. 

This means you cannot upload a track from the library as a standalone piece of audio or create a "music-only" video. The tracks are intended to support and enhance your other creative work, serving as background audio rather than the primary focus of the content.

### How to Get Started

To begin using these resources, navigate to [YouTube Studio](/youtube-tips-en/replace-song-update-youtube-suggests-10-suitable-tracks-for-you/) and locate the Audio Library section. From there, you can browse through the extensive catalog, download the tracks that fit your vision, and integrate them into your editing process. By leveraging this tool, Creators can spend less time worrying about legalities and more time focusing on their creative storytelling.
