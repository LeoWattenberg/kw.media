## Understanding Creator Music

For many video creators, finding the right balance between high-quality audio and revenue generation is a constant challenge. The introduction of [Creator Music](/youtube-tips-en/how-the-youtube-audio-library-works/) offers a solution to this dilemma by providing a dedicated space where creators can find musical pieces that enhance their storytelling without compromising their ability to earn.

### What is Creator Music?

Creator Music is an expanding library of music tracks specifically designed for use in video content. Unlike standard libraries that may only offer royalty-free options, this resource provides a growing selection of tracks that allow you to maintain the [monetization of your videos](/en/creator/). 

By integrating these tracks into your production, you can elevate the professional quality of your audio while ensuring that your content remains compliant with platform policies regarding copyrighted material.

### The Relationship Between Licensing and Monetization

The primary advantage of using Creator Music is the ability to monetize your content even when using licensed tracks. However, this benefit comes with a specific requirement: the payment of licensing fees.

To ensure that you can continue to earn revenue from your videos, you must pay the associated licensing fees upon upload. This financial arrangement serves two purposes:
1. **Compensation for Artists:** It ensures that the musicians and rights holders are paid for the use of their work.
2. **Revenue Security for Creators:** By paying the fee upfront during the upload process, you secure the legal right to monetize the video, avoiding the common pitfalls of [copyright claims](/en/youtube-tips/) that typically lead to demonetization.

### Integrating Creator Music Into Your Workflow

Incorporating these tracks into your creative process is a straightforward step in the production pipeline. Because the library is constantly growing, creators have an increasing variety of moods and genres to choose from to fit their specific project needs.

When you are ready to publish your work, the licensing process occurs during the upload phase. This ensures that the necessary permissions are in place before the video is made public, providing peace of mind that your monetization status is protected from the moment the video goes live.

By utilizing this system, creators can move away from the risk of unauthorized music use and instead adopt a sustainable model that respects intellectual property while supporting their own financial growth as content producers.
