## Understanding the "Subscribe Plus" Experiment

YouTube is currently testing a significant change to its user interface that merges two previously distinct calls to action: the subscribe button and the join button for channel memberships. This new combined element is being referred to as "Subscribe Plus."

According to YouTube, the motivation behind this experiment is to reduce clutter within the interface. The platform claims that having dual buttons—one for a free subscription and one for a paid membership—was causing confusion for users. By consolidating these into a single "Subscribe Plus" button, YouTube aims to streamline the user experience.

However, this change has raised concerns regarding clarity. While the goal is to declutter, merging a free feature (subscribing) with a paid feature (membership) into one button may actually increase confusion. From a user perspective, it becomes less clear whether they are performing a simple follow or entering a financial commitment.

For creators, this shift in UX could potentially impact how viewers interact with membership options. If you have thoughts on this interface change, it is encouraged to share feedback via the comments or directly through the Creator Insider channel to help YouTube determine if this experiment should be halted or refined.

## Autodubbing Now Available for All YPP Creators

In a more positive update, YouTube has accelerated the rollout of its [autodubbing feature](/en/video-dubbing/). While originally slated for a gradual release through the end of April, the tool is now available to 100% of creators who are members of the [YouTube Partner Program (YPP)](/en/creator/). 

It is important to note that this rollout is currently limited strictly to YPP members. If you are not yet part of the program, you will not have access to these tools at this time.

### How Autodubbing Works

The autodubbing system operates differently depending on the original language of your upload. The feature primarily revolves around English as a hub language:

*   **English-to-Other Languages:** If you upload a video in English, YouTube will automatically translate the title and description into eight specific languages: French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, and Spanish.
*   **Other Languages-to-English:** Conversely, if you upload a video in any of those eight supported languages, the system will automatically generate an English translation for the title and description, as well as provide an English autodubbing audio track.

### Strategic Implications for Creators

There is a significant limitation to be aware of: the tool does not currently support cross-translation between the non-English languages. For example, if you upload a video in German, the system will not automatically translate it into French or Hindi. 

Because of this architecture, creators looking to maximize their global reach should consider speaking and uploading their content in English. Doing so unlocks the full range of the tool's capabilities, allowing your content to be translated across all eight supported languages simultaneously.

### Autodubbing vs. Multi-language Audio

It is crucial for creators to distinguish between "autodubbing" and "multi-language audio," as they are two entirely different features within the YouTube ecosystem:

1.  **Autodubbing:** This is an automated process handled by YouTube's AI. It generates translations and audio tracks without requiring manual input from the creator once enabled.
2.  **Multi-language Audio:** This is a manual feature. To use multi-language audio, you must create a separate audio track yourself (often using external tools or voice actors) and upload that specific file to your video. This method allows for any language combination but requires significantly more effort.

## Current Status and Availability

While the rollout to YPP members is complete, there is currently no information on when autodubbing will be expanded to creators outside of the Partner Program. For the time being, the feature remains effectively a closed beta for the general public. 

Creators are encouraged to check their settings in [YouTube Studio](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-updates-ai-age-verification-autodubbing-editor-and-community-post-limits-change/) to see if the feature is active for their channel, and stay tuned for further updates regarding wider availability.
