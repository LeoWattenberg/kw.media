## AI Comment Summaries and Regional Rollouts

One of the most significant updates this week involves the implementation of [AI-generated comment summaries](/en/youtube-tips/). This feature, designed to condense large volumes of viewer feedback into digestible highlights, is already operational within the English-speaking sphere. However, its expansion into other markets—specifically Germany—is meeting some resistance.

### The Regulatory Hurdle in Europe
While YouTube intends to bring these AI summaries to the German market, the rollout is being slowed by concerns raised by the European Union. Due to these regulatory pressures, the feature is expected to remain an "opt-out" functionality for now. 

For Creators, this presents a specific challenge: currently, there is no mechanism within [YouTube Studio](/en/creator/) to disable this feature on a per-channel basis. This means Creators are entirely reliant on YouTube's platform-wide implementation and settings.

### Risks of AI Localization
The reliance on AI for comment summaries introduces potential risks regarding accuracy. While Google Translate has seen significant improvements, it remains error-prone, particularly when dealing with localized content, slang, or nuanced cultural contexts. If the AI misinterprets the sentiment or meaning of comments in a non-English market, it could lead to misleading summaries for both the Creator and the audience.

Currently, kw.media is in discussions with YouTube to advocate for a creator-side opt-out option, allowing you to decide if AI summaries are appropriate for your specific community. At this moment, however, the feature has not yet been introduced to the German market.

## Premium Membership Price Increases in India

YouTube is adjusting the pricing for Premium memberships in India, a move that has broader implications for the Creator ecosystem than it might initially seem.

### Purchasing Power and Revenue
There are significant disparities in purchasing power between different global markets. Because of this, India and Argentina have historically been regions where Premium memberships were priced much lower than in Western markets. 

From a Creator's perspective, a price increase in these regions is generally positive. When the cost of Premium memberships rises, it can lead to an increase in the overall revenue share distributed to Creators. Essentially, as the value of the subscription increases, the potential earnings from viewers in those regions also rise.

### The VPN Factor
This pricing shift also impacts users who have utilized VPNs to purchase cheaper memberships by spoofing their location to India or other low-cost regions. While this may be an inconvenience for those specific users, the overall trend of aligning prices more closely with market value is in the best interest of Creators and platform sustainability.

## Troubleshooting "Add Yours" Stickers in Shorts

Many Creators have reported issues with the [Add your Sticker](/youtube-tips-en/longer-shorts-and-add-yours-stickers-youtubes-latest-experiments/) feature in YouTube Shorts. If you find that the sticker is missing or not functioning, there are two primary areas to check: visibility settings and device performance.

### Public Visibility Requirements
According to the YouTube Help Center, the "Add Yours" sticker only functions when the user creates a **public** Short. If your video is set to private or unlisted during the creation process, the sticker functionality will not be available. Ensure your privacy settings are correct before attempting to use this interactive element.

### Technical Glitches and RAM Management
Beyond the settings, there is a known technical issue where the YouTube Shorts Creator may crash or fail to load stickers entirely. This often happens due to memory constraints on mobile devices. 

If you encounter a situation where you cannot select stickers, try completely clearing your smartphone's RAM before recording or uploading your video. Resetting the active memory can resolve these crashes and allow the sticker tools to load properly.
