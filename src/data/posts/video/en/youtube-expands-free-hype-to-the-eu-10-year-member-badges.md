## YouTube Expands Free Hype Beta and Extends Membership Badges

YouTube is rolling out two distinct updates aimed at community engagement: the expansion of the "Hype" feature into new territories and a significant extension for membership loyalty badges. While one update focuses on regional growth and discovery, the other rewards long-term viewer commitment.

## The Expansion of YouTube Hype to the EU

The free hype beta is officially expanding its reach, moving into the European Union (EU) and several other selected countries. This feature is designed to allow viewers to support creators in a way that goes beyond a standard like or comment, though its current implementation remains in a beta phase.

### Accessing Hype and the Regional Leaderboard

It is important for Creators to note that the rollout is not uniform across all accounts. Not every user will immediately see the new [hype button](/youtube-tips-en/how-does-youtube-hype-actually-work-we-have-the-data/). However, regardless of whether the button is visible on individual videos, users should be able to access their regional hype leaderboard.

Currently, finding this leaderboard can be difficult. It is relatively hidden within the interface—similar to how the training page is currently positioned—so Creators may need to guide their communities toward it if they want them to track local trends and hyped content.

### Impact on Discoverability and Monetization

For Creators wondering if the [hype feature](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-updates-new-shorts-captions-and-hype-feature-expansion/) will act as a catalyst for organic growth, the current data suggests otherwise. The hype button does not directly impact video searchability or general discoverability. Because it doesn't significantly move the needle on how new audiences find your content, it is best viewed as a "nice-to-have" community engagement tool rather than a crucial growth strategy.

Furthermore, there is a significant limitation regarding revenue for those in the EU. As of now, paid hype features are not permitted within the European Union. This means that while you can participate in the free beta and see your name on leaderboards, you cannot currently [monetize this specific feature](/en/creator/). For EU-based Creators, the [hype button](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-livestream-updates-hype-expansion-and-ai-inspiration-tools-for-eu-creators/) serves purely as a social signal rather than a financial incentive.

## Celebrating Long-Term Loyalty: 10-Year Membership Badges

While the Hype expansion is more of an experimental community tool, YouTube has introduced a concrete update for those managing long-term fan communities through memberships. YouTube membership badges now support a duration of up to 10 years, which equates to 120 months of continuous support.

### Recognizing Your Most Dedicated Supporters

Reaching the ten-year mark is a massive milestone for any viewer. It represents a decade of consistent financial and emotional investment in a channel. To help Creators acknowledge this level of loyalty, YouTube now allows the upload of a specific anniversary badge for the 10-year mark.

This update allows you to visually distinguish your most veteran members from newer supporters. If you have viewers who have been with you since the early days of memberships—some of whom may already be approaching the 90-month or 120-month threshold—this is an ideal time to [update your badge assets in YouTube Studio](/en/youtube-tips/).

### Practical Steps for Creators

If you want to take advantage of this update, consider the following:

1.  **Review Your Membership Assets:** Check your current membership badge progression and determine if you have a design that fits the 10-year milestone.
2.  **Upload Custom Badges:** Create and upload a unique anniversary badge specifically for those who hit the 120-month mark to make them feel valued.
3.  **Community Engagement:** Consider asking your community how they would like to celebrate these long-term milestones. Whether through a shout-out in a video or exclusive content, recognizing these "super-fans" can strengthen the bond between you and your most loyal viewers.

Whether you are experimenting with the new Hype beta in the EU or rewarding a decade of loyalty via membership badges, these updates emphasize YouTube's ongoing effort to deepen the connection between Creators and their audiences.
