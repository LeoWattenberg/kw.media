## New Tools for YouTube Creators: Custom Shorts Thumbnails and Super Chat Goals

YouTube continues to iterate on its feature set, introducing several updates designed to give Creators more control over their content presentation and better tools for engaging with their community during live broadcasts. From visual customizations for Shorts to new monetization milestones in live chat, these updates aim to streamline the workflow for Creators across both mobile and desktop platforms.

## Enhanced Control Over Shorts Thumbnails

One of the most anticipated updates involves how Shorts are presented to viewers. YouTube is introducing a more robust system for selecting and [customizing thumbnails](/en/creator/)—the [preview images](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-updates-end-of-year-sprint-with-mla-and-superchat-goals/) that viewers see before clicking on a Short.

### Frame Selection After Upload
Previously, thumbnail selection for Shorts had various limitations depending on the device or upload method. The upcoming update introduces a frame selection tool that allows Creators to choose a specific still image from their video to serve as the thumbnail *after* the Short has already been uploaded. 

Crucially, this functionality is being implemented for both desktop and mobile devices, ensuring a consistent experience regardless of where you manage your channel. This change removes the pressure to capture the perfect frame during the initial upload process, allowing for more thoughtful curation of how the video appears in the Shorts feed.

### Text and Filter Integration
Beyond simple frame selection, YouTube is adding a text and filter system to the thumbnail customization process. This means Creators will have the option to apply visual filters or add text overlays directly within the platform. By integrating these tools, YouTube is reducing the need for external editing software for basic preview adjustments, allowing you to make quick changes to ensure your Short stands out and accurately represents the content.

This feature set is expected to launch within the next two weeks.

## Boosting Live Engagement with Super Chat Goals

Live streaming remains a cornerstone of community building on YouTube, and the platform is continuing to expand the functionality of Super Chats. Following the recent rollout of "Replies" (last week) and "Likes" (over the past few months), YouTube is now testing [Super Chat Goals](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-creator-news-live-reminders-paid-hype-and-super-chat-goals/).

### How Super Chat Goals Work
The Super Chat Goal feature allows a Creator to set a specific financial or engagement target during a live stream. This goal is visible to the audience, creating a collective objective for the community to work toward together. 

The highlight of this feature is the reward for reaching the milestone: once the goal is met, YouTube will trigger a small animation within the chat. These visual cues serve as a celebration of the community's support and provide an immediate, rewarding feedback loop for those contributing via Super Chat.

### Current Availability
It is important to note that this feature is currently in a very small test phase. While it promises to be a highly useful tool for driving engagement and supporting Creators, a wide-scale launch date has not yet been confirmed. Once the testing phase is complete, the feature will be rolled out to more users.

## Additional Platform Updates: Ads and QR Codes

Alongside the major updates to Shorts and Super Chats, YouTube has implemented two other notable changes that affect how Creators operate and how their content is monetized.

### Side-by-Side Ads
YouTube has officially launched "[Side-by-Side Ads](/en/ads/)." While this primarily affects the viewing experience and monetization structure, it represents the platform's ongoing effort to optimize ad placements without significantly disrupting the user journey.

### Creator QR Codes
For Creators looking to grow their audience offline or across other platforms, YouTube is making Creator QR Codes available to all users over the next week. These codes provide a seamless way for viewers to find and subscribe to a channel simply by scanning a code with their mobile device, bridging the gap between physical marketing (such as at events or on merchandise) and digital growth.

## Summary of Upcoming Changes

The current trajectory of these updates shows a clear focus on three areas: visual optimization for short-form content, gamified engagement for live streams, and easier channel discoverability. Whether you are focusing on the rapid growth potential of Shorts or the deep community ties of YouTube Live, these tools provide more flexibility in how you present your brand and interact with your fans.
