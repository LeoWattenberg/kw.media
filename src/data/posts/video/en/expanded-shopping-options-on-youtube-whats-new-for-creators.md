## Expanding Your Revenue Streams with YouTube Shopping

YouTube is continuing to evolve its e-commerce capabilities, introducing several updates designed to make it easier for creators to showcase products and [generate revenue](/en/creator/) directly through their content. These updates focus on three main areas: organized product displays via Shopping Collections, a new monetization framework through the Affiliate Hub, and expanded integrations with third-party store providers.

### Organizing Products with Shopping Collections

One of the primary additions is the introduction of Shopping Collections. Rather than simply tagging individual items, creators can now curate compilations of products. These collections can include items from the creator's own linked store as well as products from other stores.

To ensure these products are easily discoverable by viewers, YouTube utilizes two main placement areas:
*   **The [Shopping Shelf](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-updates-brandconnect-expansion-and-new-streaming-features/):** This area is located directly below the video player, providing a high-visibility zone for featured products.
*   **Channel Tabs:** Collections can also be displayed as a dedicated tab on the creator's channel page, serving as a centralized storefront for their audience to browse at their leisure.

By grouping products into collections, creators can better organize their recommendations—for example, by category or theme—making the shopping experience more intuitive for the viewer and potentially increasing conversion rates.

### The Affiliate Hub: New Monetization Opportunities

For creators based in the US, YouTube has introduced the Affiliate Hub. This feature addresses a long-standing point of criticism regarding tagged products in videos and livestreams, which previously did not always offer a clear path to revenue sharing for the creator.

The Affiliate Hub implements a standard affiliate model where creators earn a percentage share when a viewer purchases a product they have tagged. Beyond direct commissions, the hub opens the door for deeper brand collaborations. Depending on the specific campaign and the willingness of the brand, creators may now be able to receive free review copies or products to test before promoting them.

#### Improving Security and Verification
Beyond monetization, the Affiliate Hub serves as a critical security layer. By routing these interactions through an official platform, it helps verify that the person requesting a product or entering a partnership is indeed the owner of the linked channel. This is a significant step in preventing common email scams and phishing attempts that often target creators pretending to be brands.

It is important to note, however, that the Affiliate Hub is currently limited to the US market. There is currently no confirmed timeline for when, or if, these specific affiliate features will expand to other regions.

### Expanded Platform Integrations for Online Stores

To give creators more flexibility in how they manage their merchandise and digital products, YouTube has expanded the list of supported store integrations. Creators can now link their channels to several prominent platforms:
*   **Shopify**
*   **Spreadshirt/Spreadshop**
*   **Spring**
*   **Forth Wall** (which specifically offers options for self-built online stores)

The addition of more providers, particularly those offering diverse store-building options like Forth Wall, is a positive development for the creator ecosystem. Increased competition among these platforms generally leads to better features, more competitive pricing, and improved tools for creators to manage their businesses.

### Summary of Impact

These updates represent a shift toward a more integrated shopping experience on YouTube. By combining curated [YouTube Shopping](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-update-new-clip-tools-shopping-sticker-upgrade-and-livestream-policy/) collections, verified affiliate opportunities, and a wider array of store integrations, creators have more tools than ever to diversify their income. Whether you are looking to sell your own branded merchandise or earn commissions through curated recommendations, these features reduce the friction between content consumption and commerce.
