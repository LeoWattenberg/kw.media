## Streamlining Shopping and Content Creation: Latest YouTube Updates

YouTube continues to refine how Creators monetize their content, localize their reach, and repurpose long-form videos into shorter assets. From significant changes in the Shorts shopping experience to new ways of generating video clips via transcripts, several updates are rolling out that impact both workflow and policy.

## Enhanced Shopping Stickers for YouTube Shorts

The process of tagging products in Shorts is becoming more streamlined to reduce friction between discovery and purchase. [YouTube Shopping stickers in Shorts](/youtube-tips-en/expanded-shopping-options-on-youtube-whats-new-for-creators/) are receiving an upgrade that changes the viewer's journey.

### Direct-to-Seller Navigation
Previously, clicking a shopping sticker would open a palette beneath the Short, providing additional information before the user could proceed to buy. In the new update, viewers will be directed straight to the seller's website upon clicking the sticker. This removes an intermediate step, potentially increasing conversion rates by sending the customer directly to the point of purchase.

### Managing Multiple Tagged Products
For Creators who tag more than one product in a single Short, YouTube has implemented a visual cue to help viewers navigate the options:
* **The Chevron Icon:** If multiple products are tagged, a chevron will appear next to the sticker. Clicking this allows the viewer to open the full list of tagged items.
* **Sticker Priority:** By default, the shopping sticker is generated from the first product in your list. However, Creators have the ability to change the order of these products, allowing you to select exactly which item serves as the primary sticker visible to the audience.

### Eligibility Requirements
It is important to note that these features are tied to the [YouTube Shopping Affiliate Program](/en/creator/). To access this program, Creators must meet two primary criteria: they must have over 10,000 subscribers and be located in one of the supported countries.

## Multilingual Audio and Thumbnail Experiments

Localization is a growing priority for global audiences, and YouTube is experimenting with how different languages are presented to viewers through [Multilingual Audio (MLA)](/en/video-dubbing/).

### Language-Specific Thumbnails
While Autodub provides automatic translations, MLA is essentially the "big brother" of this system, [allowing Creators](/youtube-tips-en/longer-shorts-and-add-yours-stickers-youtubes-latest-experiments/) to upload their own high-quality audio tracks. For those with access to MLA, a new experiment is being tested: the ability to upload different thumbnails for various languages. This allows a Creator to tailor the visual hook of a video to match the language of the audio track, which can significantly improve click-through rates in different regions.

### The Trade-off with A/B Testing
There is a technical limitation to this experiment regarding thumbnail optimization. Language-specific thumbnails are currently incompatible with A/B testing for thumbnails. Creators must choose one of three paths:
1. Use the regular, single thumbnail for all viewers.
2. Use language-specific thumbnails to match MLA tracks.
3. Utilize A/B testing to find the best performing general thumbnail.

This limitation exists because combining these features would require an excessive number of uploads—potentially three different versions for every single language track—which would complicate the upload process.

## Creating New Videos via Transcript Clipping

One of the most significant workflow updates is the introduction of "clips from transcripts." This feature allows Creators to turn sections of existing videos or live streams into standalone videos without needing external editing software.

### How the Process Works
As long as Multilingual Audio and Autodubbing are disabled for the source video, you can use the transcript to create a new asset:
1. **Selection:** You select a specific section of the text in the transcript.
2. **Automatic Timing:** YouTube automatically sets the "in" and "out" points for the video based on your text selection.
3. **Customization:** You have the option to add a 30-second intro and a 30-second outro to provide context or a call to action. (Note: To maintain viewer retention, it is often advisable to keep the intro short and skip the outro entirely).
4. **Upload Flow:** Once you click "Create Draft," you are taken through the full upload flow for a new video.

### Clarifying Terminology: Clips vs. New Videos
There is an important distinction in terminology here. Standard "clips" (created using the scissors symbol) are short snippets of a video that link back to the original. However, this transcript-based feature creates an entirely *new* video file on your channel. 

Because these are treated as new videos, they possess unique properties:
* **Recursive Clipping:** If the resulting video is still too long, you can use the transcript clipping tool on it again.
* **Re-enabling Autodubbing:** Since the clip is a new upload, Autodubbing is re-enabled, meaning you can add new language tracks to the clipped version even if they were disabled on the original source.

## Updated Live Streaming Age Policies

YouTube is adjusting its safety guidelines regarding who can broadcast live, specifically focusing on the minimum age requirements for YouTube Live.

### New Age Minimums
The minimum age required to start a [live stream](/en/live/) is increasing from 13 to 16 years old. This change is designed to enhance child safety across the platform.

### Requirements for Minors
For individuals under the age of 18 (classified as minors by YouTube), specific rules apply to prevent violations of child safety guidelines:
* **Supervision:** Minors must be accompanied by a parent or guardian during their stream.
* **Consequences:** Failure to comply with these supervision requirements may result in both live streaming capabilities and live chat being disabled for the account.

### Timeline and Impact
For adults aged 18 and above, there are no changes to how they live stream. The age increase for minors will be implemented in the coming weeks, with active enforcement following a few weeks after the initial implementation.
