## New Objection Rights for the YouTube Partner Program

Navigating the requirements and regulations of the [YouTube Partner Program](/youtube-tips-en/everything-important-about-the-new-youtube-fan-funding-partner-level/) can be challenging, and being excluded from the program is a significant blow to any Creator's monetization strategy. To provide more flexibility and fairness, YouTube has introduced a new right of objection for those facing exclusion.

Since July 31st, Creators who are notified of their upcoming exclusion from the partner program have the option to appeal this decision up to seven days prior to the effective date of the exclusion. This is a critical window of time that changes the traditional timeline of how exclusions are handled.

### The Advantage of Pre-Exclusion Appeals

The primary benefit of filing an appeal during this seven-day pre-exclusion window is continuity. If you submit your objection before the exclusion takes effect, your channel remains in the [partner program](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-get-into-the-partner-program-with-youtube-shorts/) while YouTube reviews your case. This ensures that there is no immediate interruption to your monetization or access to partner features while a decision is being reached.

It is important to note, however, that this feature is not yet available for all scenarios. YouTube is initially testing this pre-exclusion appeal process for only a small number of specific exclusion reasons. Creators will need to check their specific notification to see if they are eligible for this early objection right.

### Risks and the 90-Day Lockout Period

While appealing early allows you to stay in the program during the review, it comes with a specific consequence: if your appeal is not accepted, your channel will be removed from the partner program immediately upon the decision.

Regardless of whether you appealed before or after the exclusion took effect, the standard reapplication rules apply. If an appeal is denied—or if you were excluded and did not successfully appeal—you will face a 90-day lockout period. You must wait for this duration to pass before you can apply to join the [YouTube Partner Program](/en/creator/) again.

### Pre-Exclusion vs. Post-Exclusion Appeals

Creators have two distinct paths when facing exclusion, but they cannot use both. The system is designed as an "either/or" choice:

1.  **The Pre-Exclusion Path:** You file your appeal within the seven days leading up to the effective date of exclusion. As mentioned, this keeps you in the program during the review but leads to immediate removal if denied.
2.  **The Post-Exclusion Path:** If you do not respond during those first seven days, or if you choose not to, you still have a window of 21 days after the exclusion has taken effect to file an appeal.

Crucially, once you have filed an appeal in the pre-exclusion window, the option to file a post-exclusion appeal is voided. You must decide which strategy fits your situation best before the first window closes.

## Overhauling Channel Page Navigation and Branding

Alongside these policy changes, YouTube is implementing a revamp of [channel pages](/en/youtube-tips/). The goal of these updates is to improve the user experience and navigation for viewers visiting channels that have a limited amount of content.

### Automatic Removal of the Home Tab

For many Creators, the "Home" tab serves as the landing page for their brand. However, YouTube has determined that for channels with very little content, a dedicated Home tab may be unnecessary. 

The "Home" tab will now be removed by default if a channel meets both of the following criteria:
*   The Creator has not manually customized the Home tab.
*   The channel has fewer than 10 videos in total or has only published one type of content (for example, only Shorts).

This change is intended to streamline the viewer's experience, directing them straight to the content rather than an empty or under-utilized landing page.

### Manual Overrides and Customization

Creators who prefer to keep their Home tab regardless of their video count can manually override this default setting. To do this, you should navigate to "Customization" and then select "Channel Layout." 

YouTube is also updating the terminology within the studio. In future updates, these sections will be more clearly labeled as "Customization" or "Channel Customization," specifically referring to the management of the Home tab and overall layout.

### Consolidation into the Profile Tab

In addition to navigation changes, YouTube is simplifying how branding and channel information are managed. Previously spread across different areas, branding elements and general information are being combined into a single "Profile" tab.

Moving forward, Creators will be able to manage the following elements within the Profile tab:
*   **Channel Banner:** The header image that appears at the top of the page.
*   **Profile Picture:** The channel's primary avatar.
*   **Channel Description:** The "About" text that explains the purpose and focus of the channel.

By grouping these elements together, YouTube is aiming to make the branding process more intuitive for Creators.

Finally, it is worth noting that a small exploit has been uncovered regarding the Brand Connect Media Kit. While this does not affect general channel navigation or YPP status, Creators utilizing Brand Connect should remain aware of potential vulnerabilities in how their media kits are handled within the YouTube ecosystem.
