## Securing Your YouTube Channel Access

With many users reporting losses of account access, it is more important than ever to prioritize security when managing your presence on the platform. A common but dangerous mistake creators make is attempting to grant team members or collaborators access by simply [sharing their email address and password](/youtube-tips-en/password-protected-documents-clear-scam-warning/).

Sharing primary login credentials creates a significant security vulnerability. When you hand over your password, you relinquish total control of the account, making it difficult to track who is making changes or to revoke access quickly without changing the password for everyone involved. To avoid these risks and maintain a professional workflow, you should utilize the dedicated system for [YouTube channel permissions](/youtube-tips-en/take-off-create-your-youtube-channel-in-2024-personal-and-company-channels/).

## Managing Access via YouTube Studio

Rather than compromising your account security, YouTube provides a built-in permission system within YouTube Studio. This feature allows the channel owner to grant specific levels of access to other users, ensuring that collaborators can perform their tasks without ever needing the owner's private login details.

### How to Configure Permissions

To [set up](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-set-up-youtube-channel-guidelines/) and manage who has access to your channel, follow these steps:

1. Log into **YouTube Studio**.
2. Navigate to the **Settings** menu.
3. Click on the **Permissions** tab.

Once you are in the Permissions section, you can invite additional users by entering their email addresses. This sends an invitation to the user, allowing them to access the channel through their own separate Google account.

### Controlling Roles and User Rights

The permission system is not just about adding new people; it is designed for ongoing management. Within the Permissions tab, you have full control over the following:

*   **Adding Users:** Invite new collaborators via email.
*   **Assigning Roles:** You can define specific roles for each user, ensuring they only have the permissions necessary for their specific job (such as managing uploads or viewing analytics).
*   **Modifying Access:** If a team member's responsibilities change, you can update their role accordingly.
*   **Removing Users:** When a collaborator is no longer needed, you can instantly remove their permissions to ensure your channel remains secure.

By using this structured approach, you can cleanly manage your team and protect your account from the risks associated with password sharing.
