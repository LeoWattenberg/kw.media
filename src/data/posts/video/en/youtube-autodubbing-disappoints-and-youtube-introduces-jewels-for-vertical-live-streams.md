## The Current State of YouTube Auto-Dubbing: Quality Concerns and Risks

YouTube is continuing the gradual rollout of MLA, more commonly known as [auto-dubbing](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-news-autodubbing-on-old-videos-collabs-clarification-and-more/). While the prospect of automatically translating content for a global audience is appealing, early tests suggest that the current implementation may not be ready for prime time.

### The Quality Gap in AI Translation
Recent examples from creators testing the feature reveal significant shortcomings. The primary issue is the delivery; the dubbed audio often sounds monotonous and lacks the natural intonation of human speech. Furthermore, the actual translation quality has been described as "catastrophic" in some instances, failing to capture the nuance or accuracy of the original German source material.

For creators, this presents a dilemma. While expanding your reach to English-speaking audiences is a priority, doing so with low-quality AI audio can potentially damage your brand and alienate viewers who find the monotonous delivery off-putting.

### The Language Recognition Struggle
A recurring technical hurdle is YouTube's automated language recognition system. Currently, the platform struggles when creators produce content that mixes languages—for example, a German video that frequently uses English terms or phrases. This mixed consumption often leads the system to select the incorrect language for dubbing, creating an unpleasant experience for the viewer and a lack of control for the Creator.

### Practical Advice: To Dub or Not to Dub?
Given these issues, you should think twice before enabling [auto-dubbing](/en/video-dubbing/) on your content if the quality is not meeting your standards. If you are unsure, consider the following steps:
*   **Test carefully:** Listen to the output of the AI translation before committing it to your public videos.
*   **Use Community feedback:** Create a poll in your Community tab to ask your audience if they find the current auto-dubbing helpful or distracting.
*   **Wait for manual tracks:** It may be more beneficial to wait until YouTube allows you to upload your own language tracks. 
*   **Consider third-party alternatives:** While using external AI translation providers often involves a fee, the result is generally more professional and reasonable than the current automated offering from YouTube.

## Fixing the "Long Shorts" Display Bug

For many creators, the way "long shorts" are handled in the interface has been a source of frustration. Despite being categorized as Shorts, these videos have frequently appeared as standard long-form videos on certain parts of the platform.

### What is Changing?
The YouTube team has indicated that this display issue will be resolved in early December. Once the update is live, long shorts will be displayed correctly across:
*   **YouTube Studio:** Better management and visibility for creators.
*   **Channel Pages:** A more consistent look for viewers browsing your content.
*   **Subscription Feeds:** Ensuring subscribers see the content in its intended format.

### The Impact on Performance
It is highly likely that long shorts have performed poorly up until now because they were not being correctly served into the Shorts feed. Because the algorithm and the user interface treated them as standard videos, they missed out on the unique discovery mechanism of the Shorts player. Creators who have been testing this format should expect a potential shift in performance once the early December update ensures they are properly categorized and distributed.

## Driving Live Traffic via the Shorts Feed

In a positive move for live streamers, YouTube has fully rolled out (100%) a feature that places reminders for upcoming live streams directly into the Shorts feed.

This is a strategic advantage for Creators who already produce short-form content. By publishing clips on YouTube Shorts, you can now create a direct funnel to your scheduled live events. When you plan a live stream in advance, the platform can suggest it to viewers who are consuming your Shorts, increasing the likelihood of a higher turnout for your broadcast.

If you have not yet explored [YouTube Live](/en/live/), this is an excellent time to familiarize yourself with the tool. Integrating short-form promotional clips with scheduled long-form live events creates a cohesive content loop that maximizes viewer retention and discovery.

## New Monetization: Understanding YouTube Jewels and Rubies

YouTube is introducing a new [monetization system](/en/creator/) specifically for vertical live streams. While currently limited to US creators, this feature provides a glimpse into the future of fan funding on the platform. This system introduces a three-tier currency structure that differs from traditional Super Chats.

### The Currency Triangle: Jewels, Gifts, and Rubies
To understand how this works, it is essential to distinguish between the three different terms used in this ecosystem:

1.  **Jewels:** This is the digital currency that viewers purchase in packages. For example, a viewer might pay $8.99 for 1,000 jewels. Like many digital currencies, YouTube offers "extra" jewels depending on the size of the package purchased, meaning the price per jewel varies based on how much the viewer spends.
2.  **Gifts:** Viewers spend their purchased Jewels to send "Gifts" to the creator during a vertical live stream. These gifts are the visual representation of the tip.
3.  **Rubies:** This is the currency that the Creator actually receives. Every Gift has a fixed ruby value. The exchange rate is straightforward: **1 ruby = 1 cent**.

In short: Viewers buy **Jewels** $\rightarrow$ use them to send **Gifts** $\rightarrow$ which are converted into **Rubies** for the Creator's payout.

### How to Enable Virtual Items
For those in supported regions, this feature is managed through a new module called "Virtual Items." You can find this on the YouTube Partnership or Monetization page, situated alongside other fan funding options like Memberships and Super Chats. To begin earning Rubies, you must explicitly activate this module within your agreements and contracts.

### The Trade-off: Jewels vs. Super Stickers
There is a significant caveat to enabling this feature: **If you enable jewel gifts, Super Stickers will be disabled.** 

You cannot use both systems simultaneously; you must choose between the new Jewel/Gift system or the existing Super Stickers. When making this decision, consider your audience's habits. While Jewels offer a "gamified" experience similar to other streaming platforms (like Twitch), some creators may find that Super Chats and traditional stickers remain more interactive and enjoyable for their specific community.

As this feature rolls out further, it will be important to monitor the actual conversion rates and determine which gifts provide the best value for both the viewer and the Creator's pocket.
