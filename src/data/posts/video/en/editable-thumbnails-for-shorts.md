## Updating Your YouTube Shorts Thumbnails

For some time, Creators were limited in how they could manage their Short thumbnails; selection was only possible during the initial upload process. This meant that once a video was live, you were stuck with the frame YouTube chose or the one you picked at the start. However, as of the time this guide was published, YouTube has expanded these capabilities, allowing you to [edit short thumbnails](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-updates-brandconnect-expansion-and-new-streaming-features/) directly within the main YouTube app.

This update provides more flexibility for those looking to [optimize their visual branding](/en/creator/) and improve how their content is presented on their channel page.

## How to Edit Your Shorts Thumbnail

If you want to change the first impression of your Short after it has already been posted, you can do so by following these steps within the mobile app:

1. **Navigate to your Shorts:** Open the YouTube app and go to the section where your uploaded Shorts are listed.
2. **Enter Edit Mode:** Select the specific video you wish to modify and click "Edit."
3. **Access the Thumbnail Tool:** Tap the pen icon to open the thumbnail customization interface.

### Selecting the Perfect Frame

Once you have accessed the editor, you can scrub through the timeline of your Short. This allows you to manually search for a specific frame that best represents the content of the video or creates curiosity for the viewer. Because Shorts are fast-paced, taking the time to find a high-impact visual is essential for capturing attention.

### Adding Custom Text Overlays

Beyond simply picking a frame, the app now allows you to add text directly to your thumbnail. This is particularly useful for providing context or a "hook" that encourages users to click. To make your preview as effective as possible, you can customize the following:

*   **Size:** Adjust the text size to ensure it remains legible on various mobile screen sizes.
*   **Font:** Choose a font style that aligns with your overall channel aesthetic.
*   **Color:** Select colors that provide a strong contrast against the background frame for maximum visibility.

## Finalizing Your Changes

After you have scrubbed through the video to find the right image and added your customized text, make sure to save your changes. Once saved, the new thumbnail will be applied to your Short, allowing you to refine your content's presentation without the need to re-upload the entire video. 

Staying on top of these small feature updates can help you maintain a professional look across your channel. For more [YouTube updates and optimization tips](/en/youtube-tips/), keep an eye on the latest platform news.
