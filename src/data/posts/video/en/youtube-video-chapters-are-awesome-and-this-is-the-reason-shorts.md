## Mastering YouTube Video Chapters for Faster Navigation

[YouTube chapters](/youtube-tips-en/no-youtube-chapters-displaying/) are a powerful tool for both creators and viewers, allowing long-form content to be broken down into smaller, manageable segments. Instead of scrubbing through a progress bar to find a specific piece of information, viewers can use built-in navigation shortcuts to jump directly to the sections that matter most to them. Depending on whether you are using a desktop browser or the mobile app, there are different ways to utilize these chapters efficiently.

### Navigating Chapters on Desktop

When watching a video on a computer, the interface provides a streamlined way to view the entire structure of the content. If a creator has enabled chapters, you will see the title of the current segment displayed prominently below the video player.

By clicking on this current chapter title, YouTube opens a comprehensive list of all available chapters in a sidebar on the right side of the screen. This acts as a dynamic table of contents for the video. Rather than guessing where a new topic begins, you can scan the full list and click any chapter to jump instantly to that specific timestamp. This is particularly useful for tutorials or deep-dive videos where you may only need one specific answer from a longer presentation.

### Quick Navigation in the YouTube Mobile App

While the desktop version focuses on a visual list, the YouTube mobile app offers a gesture-based shortcut that makes navigating segments even faster. While many users are familiar with skipping forward or backward by small increments, there is a specific shortcut designed specifically for chapters.

To move between segments on mobile:
* **To skip forward one chapter:** Double-tap with two fingers on the right side of the video.
* **To skip back one chapter:** Double-tap with two fingers on the left side of the video.

This two-finger gesture allows you to traverse the video's structure without having to open a menu or manually drag the playback slider. It is an efficient way to quickly review a previous point or move straight to the next topic in the sequence.

### Improving the Viewing Experience

Utilizing these features changes how you consume content on the platform. By leveraging the chapter list on desktop and the two-finger shortcuts on mobile, you can save time and navigate complex videos with precision. Whether you are looking for a specific step in a tutorial or skipping to the conclusion of a review, these navigation tools ensure that you spend less time searching and more time watching the content that is relevant to you.
