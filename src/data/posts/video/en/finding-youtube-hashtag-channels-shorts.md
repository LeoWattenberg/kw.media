## How to Find and Access YouTube Hashtag Channels

YouTube utilizes hashtags to help users [categorize content and discover videos](/youtube-tips-en/trending-tags-are-hashtags-still-relevant/) centered around specific themes. When a hashtag is used, it effectively creates a "hashtag channel"—a dedicated landing page that aggregates all public videos using that specific tag. Understanding how to navigate these channels can significantly improve your video discovery experience.

### Searching for Hashtags via the Search Bar

One of the most direct ways to find a hashtag channel is by using the YouTube search functionality. To do this, simply go to the search bar at the top of the interface and enter the desired hashtag (including the # symbol). 

As demonstrated in our tutorial from September 2021, once you initiate the search, the platform provides a shortcut: the specific hashtag channel will appear directly below the search bar. By clicking on this result, you are taken straight to the overview page for that hashtag, allowing you to browse all related content in one place.

### Accessing Hashtag Overviews via Video Descriptions

You do not always need to use the search bar to find these aggregated feeds. You can also discover hashtag channels organically while watching videos. 

Many Creators include hashtags within their video descriptions to help with categorization and reach. If you see a hashtag in a description that interests you, simply click on it. This action serves as a direct link that redirects you to the hashtag overview page. This is an efficient way for viewers to dive deeper into a specific topic they have just encountered in a video.

### The Role of Hashtag Channels in Discovery

These hashtag channels act as discovery hubs within the YouTube ecosystem. For the viewer, they provide a streamlined way to find niche content without having to rely solely on keyword searches. For Creators, utilizing hashtags is a key part of [content promotion strategies](/en/creator/), as it ensures their videos are indexed and visible on these public overview pages.

For those seeking more technical details regarding how hashtag channels function and the guidelines surrounding them, further information can be found via [Google Support](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=de).
