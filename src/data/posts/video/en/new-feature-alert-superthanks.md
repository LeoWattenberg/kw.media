## Transcript

[Super Thanks](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-activate-super-thanks/) are now available to all Creators in the [YouTube Partner Program](/youtube-tips-en/everything-important-about-the-new-youtube-fan-funding-partner-level/). If you want to send a Super Thank, click on the "Thanks" button below a video. There, you can choose from predefined amounts and include a comment. As always with YouTube transaction revenue, you'll receive 70% after tax as a Creator.
