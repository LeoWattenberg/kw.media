## Understanding Super Thanks on YouTube

Super Thanks is a feature designed to allow viewers to support their favorite Creators directly through individual videos. While other fan funding tools often focus on live interactions, Super Thanks provides a way for fans to express gratitude and provide financial support on uploaded content.

### How Viewers Send Super Thanks

For the viewer, the process of supporting a Creator is integrated directly into the video watching experience. Below a video, viewers will find a "Thanks" button. Upon clicking this button, they are presented with several predefined monetary amounts to choose from, simplifying the transaction process. 

To make the interaction more personal, viewers can also include a comment along with their contribution. This allows the Creator to see exactly which piece of content prompted the support and read the accompanying message, fostering a closer connection between the Creator and their community.

### Revenue Distribution for Creators

When a viewer sends a Super Thank, the resulting revenue is shared between YouTube and the Creator. Following the standard model for YouTube transaction revenue, Creators receive 70% of the amount after applicable taxes have been deducted. This provides an additional [monetization stream](/en/creator/) that complements traditional ad revenue, allowing Creators to earn more from their most dedicated fans.

### Eligibility and Activation

To take advantage of this feature, Creators must be members of the [YouTube Partner Program](/youtube-tips-en/everything-important-about-the-new-youtube-fan-funding-partner-level/). As of the original publication of this update, Super Thanks are available to all eligible participants within the program.

If you are a member of the program and wish to enable this functionality for your channel, you can do so through the monetization settings in YouTube Studio. You can [activate Super Thanks here](https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UC/monetization/supers) to begin receiving support from your community on your uploaded videos. For more detailed guidance on the setup process, you can refer to the guide on [Super Thanks](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-activate-super-thanks/).
