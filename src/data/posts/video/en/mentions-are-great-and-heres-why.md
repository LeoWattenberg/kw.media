## Enhancing Collaborations with YouTube @-Mentions

[Collaborating with other creators](/en/creator/) is a powerful way to expand your reach and provide more value to your audience. To make these partnerships more effective, you can use [YouTube @-mention tags](/youtube-tips-en/what-are-youtube-handles-aliases/) to create direct, clickable links to other channels within your video's metadata.

### What are @-Mentions?

An @-mention is a tagging system that allows you to reference another YouTube channel directly without needing to paste long, cumbersome URLs into your text fields. This feature provides a clean and integrated way to credit other creators or point your audience toward a collaborator's content, ensuring that the partnership is visible and accessible.

### How to Implement @-Mentions

Adding a mention is a straightforward process that can be done when you are setting up your video or when editing existing content. You can place these tags in two primary locations: the video title or the video description.

To link another channel, follow these steps:
1. Place your cursor in the title or description field.
2. Type the `@` symbol.
3. Immediately follow the symbol with the name of the YouTube channel you want to link to.

By following this simple syntax, YouTube recognizes the tag and converts it into a functional link.

### Improving the Viewer Experience

The primary advantage of using @-mentions is the reduction of friction for your viewers. In a traditional setup, if a creator mentions a collaborator in a video, the viewer would have to manually type that person's name into the YouTube search bar to find their channel. 

With @-mentions, the process is streamlined:
* **Direct Navigation:** The mention becomes a clickable element.
* **Instant Access:** Upon clicking the tag, the user is taken directly to the target channel's homepage.
* **Increased Visibility:** Placing mentions in the title makes the collaboration immediately apparent before the viewer even clicks on the video.

### Why Use Mentions for Collaborations?

Using this feature is particularly useful during collaborations because it creates a bidirectional bridge between two communities. By making it easy for your audience to find your partner's channel, you facilitate a smoother transition for viewers moving between different creators' content. Whether you are giving credit to a guest appearance or promoting a joint project, @-mentions ensure that the linked creator is easily discoverable.
