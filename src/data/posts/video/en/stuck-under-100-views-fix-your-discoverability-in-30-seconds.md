## Five Steps to Break Through the 100-View Barrier

Hitting a plateau where your videos consistently stay [stuck under 100 views](/youtube-tips-en/why-50-hours-of-editing-gets-0-views/) is a common frustration for many Creators. Often, the issue isn't the quality of the content itself, but rather "discoverability"—the ease with which new viewers can find and click on your videos.

To move past this hurdle, you need to optimize how your content is presented and how it keeps viewers on the platform. Here are five practical steps to fix your discoverability today.

### 1. Optimize Your Titles for Search
Your title is often the first thing a viewer sees in search results. To improve your reach, move away from generic descriptions and toward searchable phrases. For example, instead of naming a video "Epic Playthrough," use a specific, intent-based title like "How to Beat Boss X." By using terms that viewers are actually typing into the search bar, you increase the likelihood of your video appearing in their results.

### 2. Create High-Impact Thumbnails
If your title gets them to look, your thumbnail gets them to click. [Thumbnails need to pop](/youtube-tips-en/how-to-not-fuck-up-your-youtube-thumbnails/) to stand out against the competition. To achieve this:
*   **Use bright colors:** High contrast helps your image stand out.
*   **Incorporate bold faces:** Human expressions often drive higher [click-through rates](/en/creator/).
*   **Keep text simple:** Avoid cluttering the image with too many words.

Crucially, always check how your thumbnail looks on a mobile device. Since a vast majority of viewers browse via phone, mobile readability is the ultimate benchmark for success.

### 3. Nail the Initial Hook
Once a viewer clicks, you have only a few seconds to convince them to stay. Avoid "boring" introductions, long animated logos, or loading screens that delay the actual content. Instead, jump straight into the action. By providing immediate value or excitement in the first few seconds, you reduce early drop-off and signal to the algorithm that your video is engaging.

### 4. Establish a Consistent Upload Schedule
Consistency helps you build a reliable relationship with your audience. By uploading on the same days and at the same times, you effectively "teach" your viewers when to expect new content. This predictability encourages returning viewers and helps stabilize your growth.

### 5. Boost Your Session Time
YouTube rewards Creators who keep viewers on the platform longer. To increase session time, stop treating videos as isolated events and start creating overarching stories. You can link your videos together using:
*   **Playlists:** Group related content to encourage binge-watching.
*   **End Screens:** Direct viewers to a specific next video that complements what they just watched.

By guiding the viewer from one video to another, you increase the total time they spend on your channel and improve your overall discoverability.
