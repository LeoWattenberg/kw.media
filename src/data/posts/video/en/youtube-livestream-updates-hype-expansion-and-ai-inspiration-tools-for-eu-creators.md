## Enhancing Vertical Live Streams and Expanding AI Tools

YouTube continues to refine its monetization and discovery features, focusing on increasing viewer engagement during live broadcasts and expanding AI-driven creative tools to more regions. The latest updates introduce "combo" options for vertical live stream gifts, broaden the reach of the [YouTube Hype](/en/youtube-tips/) system, and bring AI inspiration tools to creators in Europe.

## New Combo Options for Vertical Live Stream Gifts

For creators utilizing [vertical live streaming](/en/live/), YouTube is introducing an upgrade to the gifting system. These gifts are purchased using [YouTube Jewels](/youtube-tips-en/youtubes-end-of-year-updates-jewels-superchat-goals-and-more/), the platform's dedicated digital currency for this format.

### How Gift Combos Work
The new update allows viewers to "combo" their gifts. This means that if a viewer purchases the same gift multiple times in succession, a combo counter will appear. This counter is visible to everyone watching the stream, including the creator, providing a clear visual indicator of the level of support being given by a specific user.

Crucially, these combos are tied to the individual. Only the original gifter who started the sequence can maintain or increase the combo count; other viewers cannot "jump in" to add to someone else's existing streak. 

### Market Availability and Context
Currently, this feature—and the use of YouTube Jewels—is only available in the United States. While this brings YouTube closer to the gifting mechanics seen on other streaming platforms, creators outside the US will have to wait for further expansion. For those in the US, this update adds a layer of gamification to vertical streams, potentially increasing the incentive for viewers to send multiple gifts to climb the visibility ladder during a live session.

## Expanding Access to YouTube Hype

YouTube Hype is designed as a discovery and support mechanism. Unlike traditional monetization, Hype allows viewers to show their support for a creator or a specific video through a "hype" action. This system aggregates these actions to place creators on regional or global leaderboards, potentially exposing them to new audiences.

### New Subscriber Thresholds
YouTube is expanding access to this feature in the three markets where it is currently active: Brazil, Turkey, and Taiwan. Previously more limited, YouTube Hype will now be available to all channels within these regions that have between 500 and 500,000 subscribers. This shift targets a wide range of small-to-mid-sized creators, giving them a free tool to boost their visibility via the leaderboard system.

### Paid Hype Experiments
While YouTube Hype is primarily a free feature—granting viewers three free hypes per week—YouTube is currently testing a new monetization angle in Brazil. In this market, an experiment is underway that allows users to purchase additional hypes once they have exhausted their weekly free allowance. 

At this time, there is no confirmed timeline for when YouTube Hype will be rolled out globally beyond the current three supported countries.

## AI-Powered Inspiration Tools Arrive in Europe

One of the most anticipated updates for creators in the EU, UK, and Switzerland is the expansion of the [AI features within the YouTube Inspiration tab](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-advanced-analytics-overhaul-inspiration-ai-now-in-eu/). 

### Boosting the Creative Process
The Inspiration tab leverages AI to help creators overcome "blank page syndrome" by providing data-driven suggestions and creative prompts. For creators in these newly supported regions, the tool is intended to assist with several critical stages of video production:
*   **Writing Hooks:** Developing compelling openings to increase viewer retention.
*   **Video Structures:** Planning the flow and organization of a video to keep the audience engaged.

### Rollout Timeline
Creators in the EU, UK, and Switzerland should expect to see these features appearing in their dashboard over the next four weeks. YouTube has planned for the rollout to be completed by mid-May. Once accessible, creators can use these tools within YouTube Studio to refine their content strategy based on AI-generated insights.
