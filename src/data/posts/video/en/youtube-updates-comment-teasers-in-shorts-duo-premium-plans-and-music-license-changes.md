## New YouTube Updates: Comment Teasers, Premium Duo Plans, and Music Licensing Alerts

YouTube has introduced several updates this week that impact how viewers interact with Shorts, how users can subscribe to Premium, and—most critically for creators—how music licensing is being handled for certain labels. From new engagement tools in Shorts to AI-driven brainstorming features, here is a detailed breakdown of the latest changes.

## Enhancing Engagement: Comment Teasers in YouTube Shorts

Launched on March 27th, YouTube is rolling out a new feature designed to drive more interaction within the Shorts feed. This update introduces a "comment teaser"—a small rectangle appearing at the bottom of Shorts videos that displays a snippet or a tease of the existing comments.

For the viewer, this acts as a curiosity gap, encouraging them to dive deeper into the conversation. When a user clicks on this teaser, they are taken directly to the [comment section](/youtube-tips-en/ai-summaries-for-comments-and-why-you-dont-see-the-add-yours-sticker/), where the specific comments that were teased are positioned at the top of the list. 

For creators, this is a significant update for [community engagement](/en/creator/). By highlighting interesting or controversial comments directly on the video overlay, YouTube is making it easier for viewers to enter the discussion, which can potentially increase the overall engagement rate of a Short.

## Expanding Access: The YouTube Premium Duo Experiment

YouTube is currently experimenting with a new subscription tier aimed at two-person households. This "Duo" plan is essentially a scaled-down version of the family plan, designed for those who do not need a full family group but want more than a single account.

Currently, this experiment is limited to four specific regions:
* India
* Hong Kong
* France
* Taiwan

The rollout is gradual; approximately 50% of users in these regions will see the option. Those interested in checking their eligibility can visit [YouTube.com/premium/2person](https://www.youtube.com/premium/2person). While pricing details have not yet been disclosed, this plan represents a strategic move to capture a demographic that falls between the individual and family subscription models.

## Critical Music Licensing Changes for Creators

Perhaps the most urgent news for creators involves changes to music licensing. Two major entities—Vixen Music and Warner Music Group (WMG)—have altered their licensing terms, which could lead to content removal or revenue loss.

### Vixen Music Retractions
As of March 28th, 2025, Vixen Music has retracted its licenses from the Shorts audio library. This has immediate consequences for any creator who used Vixen music in their Shorts:
* **Shorts Removal:** If you have used Vixen music in your Shorts, those videos will be removed. YouTube is notifying affected creators via email.
* **Long-form Content:** Videos in a long-form format containing Vixen content will generally remain online, but they may receive copyright claims unless the creator has separate agreements in place.

### Warner Music Group (WMG) and Creator Music
Similarly, licenses for WMG songs obtained through YouTube's "Creator Music" tool are being revoked. These licenses are being converted into copyright claims, meaning that any proceeds generated by these videos will now go directly to WMG.

**Practical Steps for Creators:**
To avoid legal issues or unexpected revenue loss, creators should:
1. **Monitor Email:** Check your inbox for notifications regarding copyright claims or license revocations.
2. **Audit Audio:** Review the music used in your recent uploads, especially those utilizing Creator Music.
3. **Organize Paperwork:** Ensure all external licenses and permissions are documented and up to date to provide proof of right-to-use if challenged.

## AI-Powered Creativity: Updates to the Inspiration Tab

For creators located outside the European Union, the [Inspiration tab](/youtube-tips-en/youtube-updates-leaderboards-shorts-visual-guides-and-shorts-quiz-stickers/) (formerly known as the Research tab) is receiving a set of new tools designed to streamline the content creation process.

### AI-Assisted Brainstorming
The most notable addition is a brainstorming tool that leverages data and comments from your own previous videos. By analyzing what your audience is already talking about, the tool helps you generate new video ideas. Furthermore, it offers AI-assisted hooks and refinement tools to help you craft introductions that are more likely to capture attention, thereby improving overall viewer retention and engagement.

### Quick Saves
To complement the brainstorming process, YouTube has added "Quick Saves." This feature allows creators to jot down ideas as they occur or save AI-generated suggestions instantly, ensuring that inspiration is not lost before it can be moved into a production script.

While these tools are incremental rather than groundbreaking, they provide a more data-driven approach to content planning, allowing creators to generate viewer-suggested content with less manual effort.
