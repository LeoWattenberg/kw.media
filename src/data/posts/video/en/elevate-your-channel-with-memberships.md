## Understanding YouTube Memberships

[YouTube membership](/youtube-tips-en/everything-important-about-the-new-youtube-fan-funding-partner-level/) serves as a powerful crowdsourcing feature designed to help Creators deepen the connection with their community while generating an [additional stream of recurring revenue](/en/creator/). By offering exclusive perks, Creators can provide tangible value to their most loyal viewers in exchange for financial support.

## How Membership Tiers Work

To implement this feature, Creators must first establish specific payment levels and define the benefits associated with each tier. This allows the Creator to customize the experience based on the level of support a viewer is willing to provide.

### Defining Member Benefits
Benefits are the primary incentive for viewers to join a membership. According to the source material, common examples of these perks include:
*   **Customized Emotes:** Unique icons that members can use to express themselves.
*   **[Exclusive Live Streams](/en/live/):** Content accessible only to those who have paid for a membership.

### The Tiered Advantage System
[YouTube memberships](/youtube-tips-en/new-on-youtube-join-member-button-animation/) operate on a hierarchical structure. Viewers can choose from various monthly recurring subscription levels. A key aspect of this system is that higher-priced tiers are cumulative; if a viewer selects a higher level, they receive the premium advantages of that tier as well as all the benefits associated with the lower levels.

## Gift Memberships and Regional Pricing

Beyond standard monthly subscriptions, YouTube offers an additional way for the community to support both the Creator and other viewers through [gift memberships](/youtube-tips-en/how-it-works-youtube-membership-gifting/). 

### The Nature of Gifting
Unlike a standard subscription managed by the individual viewer, a gift membership is a temporary grant. It is important to note that these gifts last for only one month and end automatically once that period has expired. This provides a low-friction way for fans to introduce others to the benefits of a channel's membership.

### Regional Price Variations
Creators should be aware that membership pricing is not uniform across the globe. Prices may vary from region to region, meaning viewers in different countries will see different costs for the same membership tier based on their local currency and regional settings.
