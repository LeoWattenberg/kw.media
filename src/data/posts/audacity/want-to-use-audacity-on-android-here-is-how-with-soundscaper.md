## Transcript

Audacity is not available on Android. However, Soundscape, our web port of Audacity 4, is. To use it, simply go to soundscape.org. It has all the same features as Audacity and more, and uses a UI as close to the desktop version as possible. You can record, edit your audio, and apply effects on the go, and once you're done, export it to WAV, MP3, and more—or export it as an AUP4 file to continue editing in Audacity later.

Despite being a web app, Soundscape stores all your data on your device, and your audio is never shared with anyone.
