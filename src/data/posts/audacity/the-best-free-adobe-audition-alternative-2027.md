## Why use Soundscaper as a free Audition alternative?

### Record and mix multiple tracks

Soundscaper offers multitrack recording, non-destructive clip editing, real-time effect racks, buses, sends, and automation. For repeated takes, loop recording creates take lanes, and the comping workflow lets you choose regions and flatten the result. A project bin and nested track folders help organize larger sessions.

For a podcast or voiceover, you can arrange speech and music, use noise reduction and EQ, automate levels, and export a mix. Soundscaper can also queue stems and alternate mixes, and offers loudness normalization as part of delivery. Export options and codec availability vary by edition and format.

### Clean up recordings and use plug-ins

Both Soundscaper editions provide a spectrogram, frequency-bounded selection, a spectral brush, and profile-based noise reduction. Desktop adds plug-in hosting, including VST2 and VST3. Optional desktop models and engines also enable local speech enhancement, transcription, and source separation after installation.

Audition still has advantages for restoration: its documented toolkit includes dedicated DeHummer and diagnostics workflows. Soundscaper has no dedicated hum remover in the comparison, and its Reduce Reverb feature requires Desktop and an optional model and engine. Test your own difficult recordings before switching.

### Work in a browser or on Linux

Soundscaper Web is useful when you need to edit without installing an application; Soundscaper Desktop also offers Linux builds. Local editing in either edition needs no account. Optional sharing is separate and sends only the audio or archive you choose. Large projects are limited by available memory and storage, and browsers also impose storage quotas.

## Can Soundscaper open Adobe Audition sessions?

Soundscaper Desktop can import audio from `.sesx` sessions and produces a report of omitted items. It can also import certain legacy binary `.ses` layouts, with limitations and reports for missing media or omitted content. Soundscaper Web does not support these session imports, and Soundscaper cannot export SESX.

Keep your original session and media, read the import report, and compare the result with your Audition mix. Moving an editable session is different from exporting a finished WAV; not every setting will transfer. The [project file guide](https://soundscaper.org/docs/projects-and-data/project-files/) explains the available interchange options.

## When is Adobe Audition still the better choice?

Audition may be the better fit if you depend on its dedicated restoration tools, Premiere Pro Dynamic Link, or ambisonics. It also retains native SESX editing and export. Soundscaper is worth trying if your priority is a free editor with a multitrack mixer, browser access, Linux support, or desktop VST2 and VST3 hosting.
