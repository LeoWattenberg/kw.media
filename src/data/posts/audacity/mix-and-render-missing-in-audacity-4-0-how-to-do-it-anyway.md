## Transcript

Audacity 4.0 does not ship with a mix and render option. However, we have added this option in Sound Scaper, our web port of Audacity 4. To use it, simply import your source files or AUP4 files into Sound Scaper, select the tracks you want to mix, and go to Tracks > Mix and Render. In the dialog that appears, you now have the option of whether you want to mix, render, or both, and how many channels you want to have left at the end. I hope that helps.
